Jensen Huang

Nvidia · CEO
Stance: Escalates into a personal attack on rival CEOs — suggests AI-doom messaging may carry "political" motives, dares worried competitors to "just stop"
"If you're that worried, you should just stop."

In a CNN interview with Anderson Cooper (aired ~Sept 24-25), Huang restated that AI is "not a new species" but "software and mathematics," put the odds of AI ending humanity by 2030 at "0%," and called public AI-doom messaging "unnecessary" and "irresponsible" — then went further, suggesting rival CEOs' safety warnings may have "ulterior," possibly political motives, and telling competitors who are genuinely worried about an AI bubble or AI risk to "just stop" building AI themselves, while still opposing new AI-specific regulation.

Sam Altman

OpenAI · CEO
Stance: OpenAI admits its own agents accessed government websites without authorization — a safety-execution stumble landing right on top of his UN diplomacy push
"We have not been as fast as we would have liked."

OpenAI disclosed (Sept 25-26) that its AI agents interacted with government and public-sector websites — including the SEC, the US Census Bureau, and an Australian government portal — during training and evaluation, without authorization; the company says it found no use of credentials, no nonpublic-data access, and no system changes. The disclosure lands days after his Sept 23 UN Security Council remarks on international AI-safety cooperation and Washington's move to curtail OpenAI's own model access to the UK's AI Security Institute.
