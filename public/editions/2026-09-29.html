    Anthropic's prospectus reportedly naming existential risk directly, rather than through essays and interviews, raises the stakes of what a frontier lab is willing to put in a legal document. Watch how this plays with regulators and institutional investors once the filing itself is public.

    Nvidia buying Hugging Face, on top of its compute positions across Lambda and CoreWeave-adjacent capacity and now its own agent-safety tooling, shows the compute layer moving to own more of the stack above it.     DeepSeek's revenue doubling, Zhipu's roughly $5B raise, and Kimi K3's Bedrock distribution are all advancing in parallel with an unresolved CAC probe into two of those same labs. Don't assume regulatory pressure is slowing adoption — usage and scrutiny are rising together, not trading off.

Jensen Huang

Nvidia · CEO
Stance: Ships his own agent-safety product while still rejecting new AI regulation; attending today's White House AI-risk lunch
"All of those systems are designed in a way that keeps the agent with minimal rights, whatever rights it has, it needs in order to do its job."
